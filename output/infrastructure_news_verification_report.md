**2. India plans $125 billion push to boost high-speed road network**
*   **Project Data Accuracy Verification:**
    *   **Claim:** India plans $125 billion push to boost high-speed road network.
    *   **Verification:** Verified. India plans to expand its high-speed road network fivefold within a decade, investing ₹11 lakh crore ($125 billion). This will add 17,000 kilometers (10,563 miles) of access-controlled roads, allowing motorists to travel at speeds of up to 120 kilometers per hour. The project aims to slash logistics costs. Roughly 40% of the proposed network is already under construction and slated for completion before 2030, while work on the remaining corridors is expected to begin by 2028 and wrap up by 2033. The project will utilize a hybrid financing model, including Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) and Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM).
    *   **Source:** The Economic Times Infra (referencing Bloomberg), updated September 5, 2025.
*   **Infrastructure Confidence Rating:** 9/10 (High confidence due to verification from a highly credible Indian business news source, which also cited Bloomberg.)
*   **Recommendations for Infrastructure Story Prominence:** High prominence. This is a massive national infrastructure project with significant economic implications for India, impacting logistics, connectivity, and potentially attracting private investment.

**3. Adani Group unveils India's largest battery energy storage project**
*   **Project Data Accuracy Verification:**
    *   **Claim:** Adani Group unveils India's largest battery energy storage project.
    *   **Verification:** Verified. The Adani Group plans to build India’s largest battery energy storage system (BESS) at Khavda in Gujarat state. The proposed installation is planned for completion by March 2026 and would be India’s top battery storage facility and among the largest single-location systems globally. The group aims to scale its storage capacity to 50 GWh within five years. This project supports the group's renewable energy ambitions and is crucial for balancing large influxes of intermittent solar and wind power generation.
    *   **Source:** The Economic Times, updated November 11, 2025.
*   **Infrastructure Confidence Rating:** 9/10 (High confidence due to verification from a major Indian business publication, providing specific details about location, timeline, and capacity.)
*   **Recommendations for Infrastructure Story Prominence:** High prominence. This project is significant for India's renewable energy goals, grid stability, and positions Gujarat as a leader in energy storage. It also highlights private sector investment in critical utility infrastructure.
