Ahmedabad Metro Phase 2 is a significant expansion of the existing metro network, aimed at enhancing connectivity and easing traffic congestion in Gujarat's largest city. The project, managed by Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC), is progressing steadily with key sections nearing completion. Phase 2 comprises two corridors: Corridor 1 (Motera Stadium to Mahatma Mandir in Gandhinagar) spanning approximately 28.25 km with 22 stations, and Corridor 2 (Gujarat National Law University to GIFT City) covering about 5.42 km with 3 stations. The total estimated cost for Phase 2 is around INR 5,384 crores (approximately USD 650 million), funded jointly by the Government of India and the Government of Gujarat, with additional support from international financial institutions. Construction activities include elevated viaducts, station construction, and track laying. The project is expected to significantly boost public transportation ridership, reduce travel times between Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, and stimulate economic development along the metro corridors. Environmental impact assessments have been conducted, focusing on sustainable construction practices and minimizing disruption. The GMRC aims for partial operationalization of Corridor 1 by late 2026, with full completion of both corridors anticipated by 2028. This expansion is a cornerstone of Ahmedabad's smart city initiatives, promoting sustainable urban mobility.
